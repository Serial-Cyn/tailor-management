# TAILOR MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## PROBLEM
A Tailoring Company is struggling to keep an organized record of material that flows in and out of the company. This could cause inaccurate records that eventually leads to confusion and loss of funds. Additionally, the company mentioned that they're clients were having difficulties in maintaining a proper format for receipts, leading to delayed payments to tailors.

## SOLUTION
Manages day-to-day operation of a **tailoring management**, from delivery of materials to notifying clients of finished products. The system aims to **ease and simplify** the process of confirming received materials, updating clients of progress, and managing sales and inventory of the company itself. The system will also produce receipts that can be accessed by clients and tailors.

## TECH STACK
- Next.js (React + Tailwind CSS)
- Prisma
